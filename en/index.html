About

Woosun Planning is an AI-powered publisher founded in April 2026. We use AI to plan and write, but people decide what to publish and whether it is right.

We publish practical books for work and life. In Korea our e-books and print books are sold through Kyobo, Yes24, Aladin and Millie's Library; abroad we publish on Amazon in English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese.

About the Author

Kwangwoo Song grew up in Seoul and speaks Korean as his native language. He spent about thirty years in business consulting, working with Korean and international companies, before founding Woosun Planning, a publishing company in Seoul. He writes practical language books that pair clear explanations with AI-assisted practice.
