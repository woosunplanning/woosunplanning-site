About

Woosun Planning is a one-person publisher founded in April 2026. We use AI to plan and write, but people decide what to publish and whether it is right.

We publish practical books for work and life. In Korea our e-books and print books are sold through Kyobo, Yes24, Aladin and Millie's Library; abroad we publish on Amazon in English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese.

Our books are drafted with BookOrchestra, a multi-agent writing system that splits planning, research, writing, editing and fact-checking into separate roles. The AI that writes a draft never approves it; the final call is always human.

The press is run by Kwangwoo Song, who spent some 30 years in digital transformation consulting and founded Woosun Planning in April 2026. He grew up in Seoul and speaks Korean as his native language, and writes about using AI and running a one-person company on his Korean-language blog.
